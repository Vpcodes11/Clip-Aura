## 2024-05-24 - Accessibility for Faux Buttons and Icon-Only Actions
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like SVGs wrapped in `<button>`) and faux-buttons (like `<span>` used for modal close actions) severely degrade the experience for screen reader users and those navigating via keyboard when they lack explicit labels and semantic roles. Specifically, `<span>` elements wired with click listeners fail to receive focus or announce their purpose without `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, and `aria-label`.
**Action:** Always add `aria-label` and `title` to icon-only `<button>`s to serve both screen readers and visual users needing tooltips. For non-semantic elements functioning as buttons, always apply `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, and an `aria-label` to ensure they are fully accessible and interactive via keyboard.
